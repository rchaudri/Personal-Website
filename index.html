Myoelectric Bionic Hand

Over 6 years, I developed a myoelectric bionic hand that can be operated with signals from the human brain. Sensors on forearm muscle groups recorded electrical activity, which a program interpreted to control the hand. A 3D-printed framework with stepper motors and tendon-like strings mirrors human hand mechanics.

Neural control pipeline

  • Surface EMG sensors map intent to precise finger actuation.
  • Signal processing + intent classification translate electrical activity into grasp commands.
  • 3D-printed chassis, stepper motors, and tendon-like strings mirror human hand mechanics.
Recognition: Winner of Alameda County Science and Engineering Fair.
