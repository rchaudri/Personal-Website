An independent
engineering project

An independent myoelectric bionic hand project grew from an interest in the connection between human intent and mechanical movement.

The prototype brings together muscle-signal sensing, a mechanical hand, motors, and tendon-like strings. Its original photographs document the assembly, electronics, and a range of object configurations.

Building across disciplines

The project connected computation with physical engineering: a fabricated hand, actuated fingers, power, and electronics. Iteration meant working with both software and a real mechanical assembly.

A documentary record

The original photographs and science-fair display preserve the work as it was built. Explore the images below for a closer look at the components and hand configurations.
