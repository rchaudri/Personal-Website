The meeting-coach
MVP at SOMA

A useful meeting coach needs to examine what happens within the conversation. At SOMA, I built a real-time MVP around a custom model that scored transcripts across more than ten dimensions, including agenda setting, decision clarity, and warmth.

The model ran on-premises. GPT and Gemini integrations extended the MVP into customized coaching intended for corporate meetings and call centers.

Engineering the MVP

I developed the custom transcript-scoring model and integrated GPT and Gemini into the meeting-coach MVP. The work brought together conversation analysis and application development.

Shaping the product

Client interviews and competitive research informed the PRDs and MRDs that helped reshape the product. I collaborated with scholars in Berkeley and researchers in Europe and Asia.
