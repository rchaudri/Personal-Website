The meeting-coach
MVP at SOMA

At SOMA, I built a real-time meeting-coach MVP that analyzed transcripts across more than ten conversation dimensions, including agenda setting, decision clarity, and warmth.

I integrated existing NLP and large language models, including GPT and Gemini, to support conversation analysis and tailored coaching for corporate meetings and call centers.

Engineering the MVP

I integrated NLP and LLM capabilities into the meeting-coach application, connecting transcript analysis with tailored coaching feedback.

Shaping the product

Client interviews and competitive research informed the PRDs and MRDs that helped reshape the product. I collaborated with scholars in Berkeley and researchers in Europe and Asia.
