Two agents

At Trivium, I built and deployed two production-ready LLM agents: a CFO Agent for financial analysis and a Wildfire Agent for fire-risk exploration. Both brought together prompt refinement, application development, and interfaces for non-technical users.

I integrated GPT-4 and Gemini and refined prompts and outputs. My role combined software engineering with product management.

The financial workflow

From financial statements
to interactive analysis

The CFO Agent processed income statements, balance sheets, and cash-flow statements. It supported insights, industry benchmarking, forecasts, tailored recommendations, and dynamic graphs, with privacy controls for sensitive financial data.
